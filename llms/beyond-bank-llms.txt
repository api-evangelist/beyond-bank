# Beyond Bank Australia

> Beyond Bank Australia (ABN 15 087 651 143, AFSL/Australian Credit Licence 237856) is
> one of the country's largest customer-owned (mutual) banks and the first Australian
> bank certified as a B Corp. As an Authorised Deposit-taking Institution it participates
> in Australia's Consumer Data Right (CDR / Open Banking). Its only PUBLIC API surface is
> the unauthenticated Consumer Data Standards Product Reference Data (PRD) API — a list of
> banking products and their full detail. Consumer data sharing (accounts, balances,
> transactions) is NOT public: it requires an accredited Data Recipient under the CDR
> OAuth2/OIDC FAPI profile.

## APIs

- [Beyond Bank CDR Product Reference Data API](https://www.beyondbank.com.au/open-banking/product-api.html): Public, unauthenticated product catalogue. GET /banking/products and GET /banking/products/{productId}. Requires the x-v header (list=5, detail=7). Base: https://public.cdr.api.beyondbank.com.au/cds-au/v1
